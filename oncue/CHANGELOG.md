# OnCue changelog

## 1.0.3 (7) · 2026-11-18
Settings now has tabs: General, Calendars, Pro and About. About shows your version and links to support.

## 1.0.2 (6) · 2026-11-18
Cleaner menu: icon buttons for Settings, updates and Quit, and a shorter menu bar title. Settings opens as a normal window and closes the menu.

## 1.0.1 (5) · 2026-11-17
Checks for updates every 6 hours instead of once a day.

## 1.0 (4) · 2026-11-17
License keys from test purchases no longer unlock Pro. Downloads now come from GitHub, so Chrome no longer blocks them.

## 1.0 (3) · 2026-11-17
First release. The Join panel closes by itself when you join from your browser, Calendar or Zoom.
