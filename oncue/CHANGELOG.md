# OnCue changelog

## 1.2.2 (16) · 2026-11-19
Invitees whose invites show only an email address now get a readable name, like Ajay Anand instead of ajay.anand@company.com.

## 1.2.1 (15) · 2026-11-18
Clearer free trial: a Free vs Pro table in Settings, and a note when the trial starts and ends. When it ends, OnCue keeps working as a free app and nothing you set up is lost.

## 1.2 (14) · 2026-11-18
New: time zones. OnCue asks once where the companies and people you meet with are, then shows their local time under each meeting, copies a meeting's time for everyone, and warns you when clocks change abroad. Plus a free 7-day trial of every OnCue Pro feature.

## 1.1.3 (13) · 2026-11-18
Auto-open now also opens invites you haven't replied to (a new Joining setting, on by default), and the Join panel says why when it doesn't open a meeting.

## 1.1.2 (12) · 2026-11-18
Fixes the Join panel not appearing for a meeting that starts right after another one you just joined.

## 1.1.1 (11) · 2026-11-18
Settings has a new Joining tab for back-to-back mode, opening meetings in their apps, and opening meetings automatically, so nothing is hidden below the fold.

## 1.1 (10) · 2026-11-18
Back-to-back mode: if a call runs over, the Join panel says so and can wait until you hang up. Pro: open meetings automatically when you're free and at your Mac, and Webex meetings open in the Webex app. Recognises more meeting services, including RingCentral, Zoho Meeting and Dialpad.

## 1.0.5 (9) · 2026-11-18
Settings opens on the desktop you're on.

## 1.0.4 (8) · 2026-11-18
Settings keeps one size on every tab, and the Pro tab lists what's included.

## 1.0.3 (7) · 2026-11-18
Settings now has tabs: General, Calendars, Pro and About. About shows your version and links to support.

## 1.0.2 (6) · 2026-11-18
Cleaner menu: icon buttons for Settings, updates and Quit, and a shorter menu bar title. Settings opens as a normal window and closes the menu.

## 1.0.1 (5) · 2026-11-17
Checks for updates every 6 hours instead of once a day.

## 1.0 (4) · 2026-11-17
License keys from test purchases no longer unlock Pro. Downloads now come from GitHub, so Chrome no longer blocks them.

## 1.0 (3) · 2026-11-17
First release. The Join panel closes by itself when you join from your browser, Calendar or Zoom.
